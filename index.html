What we do

From a puppy’s first vaccine to orthopaedic surgery, most of what your pet will ever need happens here, without being sent across the city for a scan or a test.

Check-ups and vaccines

Routine care that keeps small problems small.

  • General health check-ups
  • Immunisation for puppies, kittens and adults
  • Diet and nutrition advice
  • Emergency consultations

Scans and lab tests

Answers in-house, so treatment can start sooner.

  • Digital ultrasonography
  • Radiography (X-ray)
  • High-definition microscopy
  • Blood, urine and hormone tests

Surgery

Planned and performed with gas anaesthesia for major procedures.

  • Routine surgeries, including neutering
  • Major soft-tissue surgery
  • Orthopaedic surgery
  • Gaseous anaesthesia

Critical and inpatient care

For pets who need watching after surgery or illness.

  • Oxygen therapy
  • Critical care
  • Post-op wards for dogs and cats
  • 24-hour CCTV monitoring

Travel, grooming and supplies

The paperwork and the pampering.

  • Microchipping
  • Travel health certificates
  • Pet grooming
  • Pet shop on site

When your pet has to stay overnight

Leaving them behind is the hard part. Our inpatient ward is set up so they’re never unwatched while they recover.
